MOBILE CALL SOUNDBOARD

Sound Board

Built for Android. Share your call screen with device audio enabled, then tap a pad.

Use during a call

In Zoom or Teams, start screen sharing and turn on device/share audio. Keep this soundboard visible while sharing, then play any effect below.

Android-ready
Call setup
  1. Join the Zoom or Teams call normally.
  2. Choose Share screen.
  3. Enable Share audio / Device audio.
  4. Return to Sound Board and tap an effect.

Android does not let ordinary apps inject arbitrary audio directly into another app's microphone stream. Screen sharing with device audio is the supported mobile route.
