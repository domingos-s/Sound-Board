Sound Board

Play effects locally and route them into Zoom or Teams through a virtual audio device.

Call routing

Choose your speakers/headphones below. For the call, select a virtual audio cable as the microphone input.

Use it on Zoom / Teams

  1. Install a virtual audio cable/loopback driver (for example VB-CABLE on Windows or BlackHole on macOS).
  2. Configure your OS audio mixer so this page outputs to both your headphones/speakers and the virtual cable.
  3. In Zoom or Teams, set the microphone/input device to that virtual cable.
  4. If you also need your live microphone, combine your mic + soundboard in an OS mixer such as VoiceMeeter (Windows) or Audio MIDI Setup/Loopback-style mixer (macOS).

Browsers cannot directly create a system-wide virtual microphone. The page handles playback; the OS virtual audio device handles feeding that playback into the call.
